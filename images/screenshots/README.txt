Place your actual app screenshots here.

Required files (1440×900px recommended, PNG or JPG):
  dashboard.png   →  shown on "Dashboard" tab
  orders.png      →  shown on "Order Management" tab
  reports.png     →  shown on "Reports & Analytics" tab

Tips for great screenshots:
- Use your browser's full-page screenshot tool (F12 → Ctrl+Shift+P → "screenshot")
- Or use a tool like Screenpresso, CleanShot X, or ShareX
- Crop to the browser viewport area (exclude OS chrome)
- Save at 1440×900 or 1280×800 for crisp display

Once you add the real image files, the striped placeholder cards
will automatically disappear and your screenshots will show instead.
