NordWMS was engineered from the ground up for speed, accuracy, and scale — combining real-time inventory intelligence, AI-driven demand forecasting, and mobile-first workNords into a single platform your entire team will actually love using.

Real-time Inventory Tracking

Inventory inaccuracy costs warehouses an average of 3.5% of annual revenue in write-offs, emergency replenishment, and lost sales. NordWMS eliminates that loss with sub-second stock updates across every bin, zone, and location — the moment a scan happens, your entire system knows. No nightly batch syncs. No spreadsheet reconciliation. No surprises.

  • Live stock monitoring across all locations
  • Automated low-stock alerts & reorder triggers
  • Barcode & RFID scanning support
  • Batch, serial number & expiry tracking
  • Smart zone & bin management

Multi-Warehouse

Run one warehouse or fifty — from a single login. NordWMS gives every site its own configuration, stock pool, and workNord rules while surfacing a consolidated view across your entire network. Transfer stock between locations, balance inventory dynamically, and report on every site as one unified operation.

🔗

150+ Integrations

Your tech stack shouldn't hold your warehouse back. NordWMS connects out-of-the-box with Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce, SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, and every major carrier — in minutes, not months. A full REST API and webhook engine handle anything custom, so your team never waits on an IT project to move forward.

🤖

AI Automation

Every task your team does the same way more than twice is a candidate for automation. NordWMS's rules engine lets you build intelligent triggers — auto-assigning orders by priority, routing replenishment tasks, escalating SLA breaches, and reordering stock — all without writing code. Your team focuses on exceptions; the platform handles the routine.

🔒

Enterprise Security

SOC2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant, NordWMS is built to meet the security requirements of the world's most demanding enterprises. Every byte of data is encrypted at rest and in transit with AES-256. Granular role-based access, SSO via SAML 2.0, full audit logs, and geo-redundant backups mean your data is always protected and always recoverable.

📡

<100ms Sync

Stale data causes mis-picks, overselling, and customer complaints. NordWMS propagates every scan, adjustment, and order update across all devices, locations, and connected systems in under 100 milliseconds. Whether your picker is in Aisle A or your finance team is pulling a report from another continent, everyone is always looking at the same live truth.

🌍

Global Compliance

Shipping across borders shouldn't mean a compliance headache. NordWMS has built-in support for HS code management, customs documentation, duty calculation, and country-specific labelling requirements. Whether you're shipping from a bonded warehouse in Singapore or a 3PL in the Netherlands, the platform ensures every shipment is compliant before it leaves the dock.

About Us

Built by warehouse experts,
for warehouse experts

Founded in 2019, NordWMS was born out of frustration. Our founding team spent a combined 50+ years running warehouse operations for global retailers and 3PLs — watching millions of dollars evaporate through picking errors, inventory drift, and software that hadn't meaningfully evolved since the early 2000s. We didn't set out to build another WMS. We set out to build the platform we'd always wished existed: fast, intelligent, genuinely usable on the floor, and priced so that mid-market businesses could actually afford it.

That vision resonated. Today we're a 120-person team spread across 4 continents, and our platform processes over 10 million orders every single month for 500+ companies — from family-run fulfilment centres to enterprise 3PLs managing multiple million-square-foot sites. We're profitable, founder-led, and singularly focused on making your warehouse the most efficient operation in your industry.
